# Skill: Production Deployment & Google Search Console

Load for any go-live: building for prod, deploying to a cloud host, wiring DNS +
SSL, and getting the site indexed. This is the end-to-end launch runbook; the
`production-launch-checklist.md` template is its interactive companion.
Deploying itself and buying domains/plans are **Always-Stop** — you prepare
everything and the Founder presses the button.

Full doctrine, checklists, and failure modes: `production-deployment-gsc-reference.md`.
