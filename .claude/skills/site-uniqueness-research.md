# Skill: Site Uniqueness & Signature-Feel Research

Load at the START of any website design/redesign, before motion or layout is
built — to make the site feel distinctive and alive rather than templated.
Pairs with `motion-design.md`, `web-design-rules.md`, and `deep-research.md`.
Output feeds the grooming report, the motion plan, and the asset manifest.

Full doctrine, checklists, and failure modes: `site-uniqueness-research-reference.md`.
