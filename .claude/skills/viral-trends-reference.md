# Reference: viral-trends

Deep detail for `viral-trends.md`. Read this when actually implementing — not to decide whether the skill applies (the lean file + `skills/INDEX.md` answer that).

## Trend research workflow (always search — trends die in days)
1. Web-search platform-native sources for the client's niche: TikTok Creative Center, Google Trends (rising, not top), X/Reddit rising threads, YouTube trending in category, competitor posts from the last 7 days.
2. Score each trend: relevance to client (does it map to a content pillar?) × window remaining (most formats have a 48–72h prime window; audio/meme formats shorter, search-driven topics longer) × brand safety (would the client's customer cringe?).
3. Never fabricate trend data or stats — every claim in trend reports carries a source link and a date stamp.

## Viral mechanics (what actually moves the needle)
- **Hook in the first 1–3 seconds / first line** — the scroll-stop is the whole game: pattern interrupt, bold claim, open loop, or specific number.
- **Share/save triggers**: utility (steal this checklist), identity (this is SO us), emotion (awe > anger > humor for brand accounts), social currency (be first to show your friends).
- **Platform-native formats**: short vertical video (15–45s), carousel (7–10 slides, one idea per slide, last slide = CTA), X/LinkedIn thread (hook tweet must stand alone), single-image + caption story.
- **Open loops across a series** beat one-offs — end with the next episode's question.

## Idea generation engine
- Per client: 3–5 **content pillars** (from `company/business-context.md`) × recurring **formats** × current **trend remixes** = the idea matrix.
- Maintain `company/content-ideas.md`: append idea cards, never delete (mark used/expired).
- **Idea card format**: `hook · format · platform · pillar · effort (S/M/L) · trend window (evergreen or date) · source link`.
- Batch output: when asked for ideas, deliver 10–20 cards spread across pillars and effort levels, not 3 polished ones.

## Guardrails
Ideas and drafts only — publishing to real channels, boosting, or paid promotion is Always-Stop. No engagement-bait that damages long-term brand trust (rage bait, false claims, fake giveaways).
