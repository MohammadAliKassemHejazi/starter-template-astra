# Skill: Motion Design & Web Animation

Load for any animation or real-time visual work — page transitions, scroll effects,
micro-interactions, hero motion, SVG/illustration animation, and purposeful 3D/WebGL
experiences. Governs the motion and 3D engineers (framer-motion, GSAP, Three.js)
and pairs with `web-design-rules.md` (personality dictates motion) and
`css-architecture.md` (performance). Motion is what makes a site feel alive; bad
motion makes it feel broken.

Full doctrine, checklists, and failure modes: `motion-design-reference.md`.
